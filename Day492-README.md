# Quality Measure CPT II Codes Based on Diagnosis Codes – Dr. Atluri

## 1. Issue / Request

A request was received regarding the **Quality Measure CPT II codes** populated for patients associated with specific **DX (ICD-10) codes**.

The request was to identify, for patients seen from **June 2026 onward**, which of the following CPT II / quality measure codes were populated based on the diagnosis codes provided in the attachment:

* 1123F
* 1124F
* 1123F-8P
* G8420
* G8417
* G8418
* G2181
* G9716
* G8421
* G8419
* G8427
* G8430
* G8428

The purpose of the request was to determine whether the applicable quality-measure codes were being appropriately populated for the patients with the specified diagnoses.

---

## 2. Investigation

The provided DX codes and the corresponding CPT II codes were reviewed.

The CPT II codes are **auto-populated based on the applicable diagnosis codes and the associated quality-measure intervention/documentation performed for the patient**.

In particular, the applicable quality-measure coding includes interventions such as the **Diabetes Eye Exam**, including the **Retinal Examination** test.

Therefore, the presence of a CPT II code is dependent not only on the patient's diagnosis but also on the applicable intervention/documentation associated with the quality measure.

---

## 3. Finding

The review confirmed that the mentioned **CPT II codes are auto-populated based on the applicable DX codes and the corresponding quality-measure interventions performed/documented for the patient**.

For the Diabetes-related diagnoses, the **Diabetes Eye Exam / Retinal Examination** intervention is one of the applicable clinical activities that can result in the corresponding quality-measure CPT II coding.

The attached report contains the details of the reviewed DX codes and the corresponding CPT II codes.

---

## 4. CPT II Population Flow

The general flow is:

```text
Patient Encounter
       |
       v
Applicable DX / ICD-10 Code
       |
       v
Quality Measure Eligibility / Mapping
       |
       v
Applicable Intervention
       |
       +---- Diabetes Eye Exam
       |          |
       |          v
       |    Retinal Examination
       |
       v
Quality Measure CPT II Code
       |
       v
CPT II Code Auto-Populated
```

---

## 5. Example

For a patient with an applicable Diabetes diagnosis:

```text
DX Code
   ↓
Diabetes-related Quality Measure
   ↓
Diabetes Eye Exam
   ↓
Retinal Examination
   ↓
Applicable CPT II Code
```

The CPT II code is therefore generated/populated according to the applicable DX and the quality-measure intervention documented for the patient.

## 7. Conclusion

The reported CPT II codes are not populated solely based on the diagnosis code. They are associated with the applicable **quality-measure configuration, diagnosis, and documented intervention**.

For the reviewed Diabetes-related cases, the **Diabetes Eye Exam / Retinal Examination** intervention is relevant to the CPT II code population.
