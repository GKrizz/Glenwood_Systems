## 🔍 Case Overview

Kurup Wound Care requested assistance with **MIPS reporting** as their submitted Healthmonix reports showed **missing and incomplete data**.
The client was nearing the reporting deadline and needed urgent guidance on:

* Entering required data correctly in **Glace EHR**
* Understanding which MIPS measures are supported
* Identifying possible workarounds for unsupported measures

---

## 🚩 Key Issue Identified

There was an **expectation mismatch** between selected MIPS measures in Healthmonix and **Glace EHR eCQM support**.

* **13 measures** were selected in Healthmonix
* **Only 8 measures** are eCQM-supported in Glace
* **4 measures are NON-eCQM** and cannot be generated or supported by Glace

This clarification was critical to resolving the case.

---

## ✅ Supported eCQM Measures (Guidance Provided)

Detailed **step-by-step documentation guidance** was prepared and shared for the following **8 supported measures**:

1. **Diabetes: Glycemic Status Assessment > 9%**
2. **Documentation of Current Medications**
3. **Depression Screening & Follow-Up Plan**
4. **Tobacco Use: Screening & Cessation Intervention**
5. **Screening for High Blood Pressure & Follow-Up**
6. **Controlling High Blood Pressure**
7. **Falls: Plan of Care**
8. **Closing the Referral Loop: Receipt of Specialist Report**

📌 Guidance included:

* Measure eligibility criteria
* Required documentation locations in Glace (Assessment, Vitals, Investigation, Plan, MUPerformance, Referral, etc.)
* Measure logic (MET / NOT MET conditions)
* Common pitfalls (e.g., diagnoses entered only in Superbill not counting for MIPS)

---

## ❌ Unsupported NON-eCQM Measures

The following measures are **not supported by Glace EHR** and **no reports/workarounds can be provided**:

* Advance Care Plan
* Screening for Social Drivers of Health
* Preventive Care & Wellness (Composite)
* Adult COVID-19 Vaccination Status

This limitation was clearly communicated to avoid further confusion.

---

## 📤 Resolution & Outcome

* Required reports for supported eCQM measures were **generated and sent** by the Technical Support team
* Comprehensive documentation guidance was **shared internally**
* Client expectations were aligned regarding **system limitations**
* Case was successfully **closed without pending actions**

---

## 🧠 Key Takeaways

* Always confirm **eCQM support** before measure selection
* Healthmonix selection ≠ EHR reporting capability
* Proper documentation location in Glace is **critical for MIPS compliance**
* Early expectation setting prevents last-minute escalations
